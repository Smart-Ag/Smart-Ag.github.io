## Technologies for a sustainable future

Our mission is to apply advanced ideas of robotics, AI, machine vision, remote sensing, data mining and information technology to smart agriculture.

## Core Techniques

The core techniques include 2D/3D image processing, multispectral/hyperspectral imaging, spectroscopy, deep learning, machine learning, digital mapping, biochemical sensing, etc.

## Research Instruments

The tools available for carrying out our research are unmanned aerial vehicles, unmanned ground vehicles, video cameras, multispectral cameras, hyperspectral cameras, DGPS, and various electrical, optical and chemical sensors.
